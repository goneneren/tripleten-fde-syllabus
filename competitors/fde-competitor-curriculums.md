## Cross-provider topic map

- LLM API integration and prompt basics
  - Evidence strength: strong
  - Providers: Udacity, Correlation One, DataCamp, General Assembly
  - Instructor implication: Tier 1 should have practical AI fluency, not only traditional backend review skill
- Structured outputs and tool/function calling
  - Evidence strength: strong
  - Providers: Udacity, DataCamp, DeepLearning.AI
  - Instructor implication: Tier 1 should understand common engineering risks; Tier 2 should judge production tool execution safety
- RAG and vector retrieval
  - Evidence strength: strong
  - Providers: Udacity, AI Makerspace, DataCamp, DeepLearning.AI, General Assembly
  - Instructor implication: Tier 2 should assess retrieval design, grounding, evals, and failure modes
- Agentic workflows
  - Evidence strength: strong
  - Providers: Udacity, AI Makerspace, DataCamp, DeepLearning.AI, General Assembly
  - Instructor implication: Tier 2 should review agent architecture, state, permissions, retries, and observability
- Evaluation and observability
  - Evidence strength: strong
  - Providers: Udacity, AI Makerspace, DeepLearning.AI, DataCamp, General Assembly
  - Instructor implication: Tier 2 must evaluate whether tests/evals catch regressions rather than demonstrate happy paths
- Production deployment and MLOps
  - Evidence strength: strong
  - Providers: Springboard, General Assembly, Udacity, AI Makerspace
  - Instructor implication: Tier 1 needs deployment hygiene; Tier 2 needs reliability and operations judgment
- Enterprise/client capstones
  - Evidence strength: medium
  - Providers: Correlation One, Springboard, AI Makerspace, General Assembly
  - Instructor implication: Tier 3 should own client scoping, delivery judgment, capstone defenses, and stakeholder evaluation
